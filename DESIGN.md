# Design Direction: KaiGoBiz (Modern Fintech Slate)

> Reading this as: Merchant Payment Gateway & Scraper Dashboard for merchants & operators, in a Modern Fintech Slate style (precision matte dark, crisp typography, restrained emerald & teal accents, zero gratuitous glow/blur), dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## 1. Dials
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)** - Professional, trustworthy, confident payment infrastructure interface.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Structured & Dynamic)** - Distinct hierarchical cards for auth, checkout playground, and live transaction ledger.
- **MOTION: 1 (Calm / Subtle)** - Focused hover states, smooth micro-transitions (150ms), no endless floating loops.

## 2. Palette
- **Base Background**: `#0b0f19` (Deep Slate Charcoal)
- **Card Surfaces**: `#131b2e` (Matte Solid Slate)
- **Card Borders**: `#1e293b` (Subtle Slate Border)
- **Input Backgrounds**: `#0d1526`
- **Text Primary**: `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100, high contrast 14:1)
- **Text Muted**: `#94a3b8` (Slate 400, contrast 6.5:1)
- **Text Dim**: `#64748b` (Slate 500)
- **Accent Primary**: `#0284c7` (Fintech Sky Blue) / `#0ea5e9`
- **Accent Success**: `#10b981` (Emerald Green)
- **Accent Warning/Danger**: `#ef4444` (Ruby Red)

## 3. Typography & Spacing
- **Font Family**: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, sans-serif
- **Scale**:
  - Title: 20px / 600 weight
  - Body: 14px / 400 weight
  - Small / Labels: 12px / 500 weight
  - Monospace (Codes & IDs): 13px ui-monospace, 'JetBrains Mono', monospace
- **Border Radii**:
  - Buttons & Inputs: 8px
  - Cards: 12px
  - Badges: 6px

## 4. Craftsmanship Rules
- No decorative emojis in headings, labels, or button text.
- Clean inline SVG icons with matching stroke weights.
- Visible, high-contrast keyboard focus indicators (`:focus-visible`).
- Accessible table with sticky headers and clean row dividers.
